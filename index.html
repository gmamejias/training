Weekly target

  • 3 gym sessions (Tue, Thu, Sat)
  • 2 badminton sessions (any 2 of Mon, Wed, Fri)
  • 1 Session C on the Mon/Wed/Fri day without badminton. Extra Session C on any other evening is fine.

Why it's laid out this way

  • Gym and badminton never share a day, so evenings stay short and legs stay fresh for the stairs.
  • Legs are trained hard once, on Saturday. Soreness lands on Sunday and is mostly gone by Monday's court session.
  • Badminton already loads your legs twice a week, so one leg day is enough for recomposition.
  • Session C is gentle and safe close to bedtime. It helps recovery and doesn't tire you for the next day.

How to use this app

  • The app opens on today's session. Enter the weight (kg) and reps for each set, then tap Save session.
  • Each exercise shows what you did last time. A green line means you're ready to go heavier.
  • Unsaved entries are kept as a draft if you close the app mid-workout.
  • When you enter the reps for a set and tap away, a rest timer starts at the bottom of the screen with the right rest for that exercise. It beeps when rest is over. After the last set it times your move to the next exercise.
  • Check the Progress tab for your week, lift trends, body weight and history. Back up your data there once a week.

Badminton

On court days

  • Warm up 5–8 min before play: light jog in place, leg swings, shoulder circles, a few easy lunges.
  • No extra training on these days. Badminton is your conditioning.
  • Afterward: 5 min of calf and hip-flexor stretching (moves 2 and 8 from Session C).

Sunday

Full rest. Batch-cook day. A walk is fine.

How to progress

Double progression

  • Pick a weight you can lift for the bottom of the rep range with 1–2 reps left in reserve.
  • Each week, add a rep where you can. When every set hits the top of the range, raise the weight by the smallest step available and start back at the bottom. The app flags this in green.
  • For barbell lifts, log the total weight: bar plus all plates. For dumbbells, log the weight of one dumbbell. For machines, log the number on the stack.
  • For bodyweight and timed moves, progress by adding reps or seconds, then slow the tempo.

When to back off

  • Legs still sore on Monday morning: cut Saturday to 2 sets per exercise next week.
  • Shoulder ache after a heavy badminton week: drop the shoulder press to 2 sets and keep face pulls.
  • Every 6–8 weeks, take one lighter week at about half the sets.
  • Missed a gym day: do the next one in order. Don't double up.

Session C progression

  • Weeks 1–2: follow the listed times.
  • Weeks 3+: add 15 s to holds and 2 reps to moving drills, or go a little deeper while keeping it pain-free.
  • Keep it gentle. Session C should leave you feeling looser, never tired.

Food on training days

  • Aim for a protein source at every meal: sardines, eggs, tofu and monggo all count.
  • A snack 60–90 min before training (yogurt with muesli, or a banana) helps evening energy.
